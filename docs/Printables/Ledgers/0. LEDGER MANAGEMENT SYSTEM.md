### Daily Ledger Procedures
1. **Start of Shift:**
    - Verify previous entries are complete
    - Check page numbers and continuity
    - Initial at top of new page
        
2. **During Shift:**
    - Record entries in real-time
    - Use clear, legible handwriting
    - Cross-reference with other ledgers when applicable
        
3. **End of Shift:**
    - Complete all partial entries
    - Sign at bottom of last entry
    - Note total entries for the shift
    - Store in designated location
