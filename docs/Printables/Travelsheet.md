## TRAVELSHEET PROTOCOL

### Single SKU Rule:
- **One travel sheet = One SKU = One Lot Number**
- Multiple SKUs require separate travel sheets
- Lot numbers increment per SKU per day (LOT-TGLM-YYYYMMDD-0001, LOT-TGLM-YYYYMMDD-0002, etc.)

### Flow:
1. **Supervisor** → Creates travel sheet with SKU-specific planning
2. **Ingredient Prep** → Records ING-Lots (5 rows available for multiple ingredients)
3. **Process Teams** → Record each step with start/end times and PIC (4 rows for shifts)
4. **Packaging** → Records packaging details for this SKU
5. **QC** → Verifies quality with rejection rate tracking
6. **Supervisor** → Final sign-off with performance metrics

### Notes:
- Stoppages recorded in separate Stoppage Ledger (reference by lot number)
- Warehouse uses separate ledgers (not involved in travel sheet)
- Each shift completes their row in process steps
- Travel sheet stays with the product until completion
